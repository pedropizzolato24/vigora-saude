# Goldies Saúde Integrada: competitive intel for Vigora positioning

**Headline finding: Goldies is a direct B2C competitor to Vigora — not a B2B2C play in a different channel — but it does *not* close the dead man's switch gap and its pricing is exactly 2× Vigora's.** Goldies charges **R$9,90/month** (basic) or R$104,90/year and is sold self-service through the app stores with a 30-day trial. It has no insurer, clinic, pharmacy, or senior-care partnerships. The "Como estou me sentindo" button is a *manual* user-initiated status update — not a scheduled check-in that automatically escalates when the user fails to respond. Goldies has roughly **1,500 downloads** and **2 App Store reviews** after ~5 years of development, leaving the elderly check-in / dead man's switch position in Brazil functionally unclaimed.

---

## 1. Pricing — fully public, freemium consumer SaaS

Goldies has a transparent public price list. There is no "request a demo" gate or sales-led motion.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (effective monthly) | Annual total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Básico** (1 monitorado) | R$9,90 | R$8,74 | **R$104,90/year** |
| **Família** (up to 6 people) | R$29,90 | R$26,66 | **R$319,90/year** |
| **30-day free trial** | — | — | All tiers |
| **Healthcare professionals** | Free | Free | Free |

Source: [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/planos)

**Key implications for Vigora:**
- Vigora at **R$4,90/month is exactly half Goldies' R$9,90/month**, and Vigora's R$29,90/year is ~28% of Goldies' R$104,90/year. The price gap is large enough to be a real positioning lever for budget-conscious consumers.
- Goldies offers **no lifetime tier**. Vigora's R$99,90 lifetime price is structurally different — it competes with ~1 year of Goldies Basic at the same dollar amount.
- The "free for health professionals" tier suggests Goldies is using doctors, physiotherapists, and personal trainers as network-effect recommenders, not as a paid B2B channel [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/).

## 2. Distribution model — direct B2C, no insurer or clinic channel

Goldies sells direct-to-consumer through the app stores with self-service signup. There is no evidence of B2B2C distribution.

- App Store and Google Play self-service signup confirmed at [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/goldies-sa%C3%BAde-integrada/id1594766763) and [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.goldies.app).
- The `/empresas` page does not exist (returns 404), indicating no enterprise sales motion.
- The official "Parceiros" page lists only **lifestyle/benefits providers** — Uppo (digital benefits bank), Campos Humana (mental health), Kikos Fit (fitness), Fiibo (health marketplace), D-Gym (digital gym) — and contains **zero health plans, clinics, hospitals, pharmacies, or long-term care facilities** [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/parceiros).
- LinkedIn positions Goldies' target audience as **"filhos de idosos"** (adult children of elderly parents), confirming a consumer-paid model where the adult child is the buyer [linkedin](https://br.linkedin.com/company/goldies-saude-integrada).

**Decision implication:** Goldies and Vigora are competing for the same buyer (the adult child managing an elderly parent), in the same channel (app store self-service). This is *not* a "different channel" play. The competitive question is therefore about feature differentiation and price, not distribution.

## 3. Feature comparison — Goldies vs. Vigora

| Feature | Goldies | Vigora | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dead man's switch (scheduled check-in with auto-escalation if no response)** | **No** — only manual "Como estou me sentindo" button | **Yes** | This is Vigora's clearest differentiation. Goldies' button requires the elder to take action; Vigora's escalates on *inaction*. |
| **Medication reminders** | Yes, with simultaneous notification to caregiver | Yes | Goldies has no documented persistent alarms (continues until acknowledged), no missed-dose escalation, and no drug interaction warnings [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.goldies.app) |
| **SOS button** | Sends in-app notification to authorized contacts | Yes | Goldies' SOS is message-only — no SMS, no voice call, no GPS location share, no monitoring center, no photo/video [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/goldies-sa%C3%BAde-integrada/id1594766763) |
| **Medical record / document storage** | Yes — photo upload of exams with auto-digitalization, or lab login to pull results; medication list; vaccines; allergies | Yes | Goldies has the more developed flow (lab credential pull is a real feature) [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/) |
| **Caregiver-facing view** | Yes — alerts and reports sent to "cuidadores e parentes"; family plan supports up to 6 monitored people; separate physician module for doctors with patient consent | Per Vigora canvas: "Cuidador" is secondary user type | Goldies has invested heavily in multi-user architecture |
| **Health indicators / graphs** | Yes | Yes | Parity |
| **Vaccine history / allergies** | Yes | Not confirmed | Goldies edge |

**On the dead man's switch specifically — high confidence finding from two independent investigations:** Both executors examined Goldies' homepage, app store descriptions, demo video page (`/conteudos/videos/conheca-o-app-da-goldies/`), pricing page, and LinkedIn page. No Goldies-controlled source uses the language "check-in diário," "confirmação de bem-estar," "alerta de inatividade," or "se não responder." The only check-in–adjacent feature is **"Como estou me sentindo"** — a button the elder taps to declare their state, with family notified only when the user changes the status. This is the opposite mechanic from a dead man's switch: it requires action to trigger an alert, rather than inaction [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/) [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.goldies.app).

**Note on Goldies' strengths:** Where Goldies does outperform Vigora is in (a) **medical record depth** — lab credential integration is a real feature Vigora doesn't claim, and (b) **caregiver-side maturity** — the family plan for up to 6 monitored people and a separate physician module are more developed than Vigora's current "Cuidador as secondary user type" framing.

## 4. Traction — small, persistent, but not commercially validated

Goldies has been operating for roughly 5 years (Forbes Brazil mentioned it in a 2021 healthtech roundup [forbes](https://forbes.com.br/forbes-tech/2021/07/16-healthtechs-que-estao-revolucionando-a-saude-para-ficar-de-olho-em-2021/)) but the numbers are small:

- **~1,500 total downloads** (self-reported on pricing page) [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/planos)
- **2 reviews on Apple App Store**, 5.0 stars average — far too few to be meaningful [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/goldies-sa%C3%BAde-integrada/id1594766763)
- **7 employees** on LinkedIn, **607 followers** [linkedin](https://br.linkedin.com/company/goldies-saude-integrada)
- **4 co-founders** — Glauber Fonseca (CEO), plus undisclosed commercial, technical, and medical leaders [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/sobre/)
- **No funding rounds publicly announced**. No seed, Series A, or angel-round press coverage located.
- **Accelerator program acceptances**: Founder Institute, Microsoft for Startups Hub, CIETEC/USP (2024–2025), Inova HC (2024–2025) [cietec](https://cietec.org.br/empresas/goldies-saude-integrada/) [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/sobre/)
- **Active development**: App Store version 1.34.6 updated March 30, 2026; nearly weekly releases [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/goldies-sa%C3%BAde-integrada/id1594766763)
- **Location**: Barueri, São Paulo [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/politica-de-privacidade/)
- **Press mentions**: Forbes Brazil (2021 healthtech roundup), included in Distrito.me and Futuro da Saúde senior-tech listings, Conexão 60 (Rede Longevidade) [redelongevidade](https://redelongevidade.org.br/conexao60/iniciativa/goldies-saude-integrada/)

**Reading the traction:** A 5-year-old healthtech with ~1,500 downloads and no funding round is not a runaway competitor. The active weekly development and accelerator participation suggest the team is committed but hasn't found a repeatable acquisition channel — which is exactly the problem Vigora must also solve, just with less prior investment. Goldies' presence in CIETEC/USP and Inova HC (Hospital das Clínicas innovation arm) hints they may *want* to land institutional channels, but no partnership has materialized as of this research.

## 5. Weaknesses — limited review data is itself a finding

The most important weakness signal is the **absence** of public review data. With only 2 App Store reviews and inaccessible Google Play review counts, there is no observable complaint pattern in public sources. No Reddit threads, blog reviews, or independent reviewers discussing Goldies were located. This means:

- **Goldies has not reached the scale where organic word-of-mouth or review-driven discovery operates.** A direct competitor with only 2 reviews after 5 years has not validated product-market fit.
- **Curated testimonials on the homepage** are the only user voice available, which by definition reflects the most favorable cases [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/).
- **The dead man's switch absence is itself a weakness** — for a product positioned at adult children worried about elderly parents living alone, the lack of an inactivity escalation feature is a meaningful gap that no current competitor has filled.

This is a low-confidence finding on operational/UX weaknesses (insufficient data), but a **high-confidence finding on commercial weakness** (5 years of work, ~1,500 downloads, no funding).

## 6. Strategic implications for Vigora

**The dead man's switch is still uncontested.** Two independent investigations of Goldies' marketing, app store listings, demo video, and feature descriptions found no evidence of a scheduled check-in with auto-escalation. The Whitespace 1 position identified in the prior competitive landscape ("the check-in app that also handles medications") remains open. Vigora should lead marketing with this feature, not bury it among five.

**Goldies competes head-on but at 2× the price and with weaker traction.** Same channel (app store self-service), same buyer (adult child), overlapping feature set minus the check-in. Vigora's R$4,90/month is a 50% discount to Goldies' R$9,90, and the R$99,90 lifetime tier has no Goldies equivalent. Price is a real lever.

**Goldies is stronger on caregiver-side architecture.** Multi-monitored family plan (6 people), a separate physician module with patient consent, and lab-credential integration for exam pulling are more developed than what Vigora has documented. If Vigora wants to win the caregiver buyer, these are the catch-up features to prioritize. If Vigora wants to win the elderly monitorado as primary user, the dead man's switch + cheaper price story is sufficient differentiation.

**There is no "Goldies has the insurer relationships, we can't compete" risk.** Goldies has zero health plan, clinic, or pharmacy partnerships. If Vigora's stepdad's network produces even one operadora pilot, Vigora leapfrogs Goldies on distribution.

**The "free for health professionals" tier is worth copying.** Goldies has identified that doctors and physiotherapists are influential recommenders for elderly patients. A free tier (or a paid tier with reseller commission) for healthcare professionals could replicate this network effect for Vigora — possibly faster than Goldies has, given Vigora's lower consumer price point making conversion easier.

## Where additional research would most strengthen the conclusions

- **Google Play rating and review count for Goldies.** The App Store has only 2 reviews, but Google Play likely has more (the install range field would reveal a real download band). This would let us confirm or contradict the ~1,500 download claim and surface any complaint patterns.
- **Whether Goldies is currently in any operadora or hospital pilot via CIETEC/USP or Inova HC.** These accelerators have direct lines to Hospital das Clínicas and the USP medical ecosystem. If Goldies converts one of these into a paid pilot before Vigora launches, the competitive picture shifts. A LinkedIn search of Goldies' team for "operadora" or "Hospital" job titles would surface early signals.
