# Who actually installs and pays for a Vigora-style app in Brazil — and what reaches them

**Headline finding: The buyer is the adult child, not the idoso.** Every meaningful piece of evidence — Goldies' explicit "filhos de idosos" positioning [linkedin](https://br.linkedin.com/company/goldies-saude-integrada), customer testimonials structured around the daughter as decision-maker [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/), the IBGE finding that 59.3% of 60+ Brazilians who don't own a phone cite "inability to use it" as the reason [ibge](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/44032-no-brasil-88-9-da-populacao-de-10-anos-ou-mais-tinha-celular-em-2024), Procon's 2025 finding that 54% of elderly struggle to use apps for shopping/services [portalcbncampinas](https://portalcbncampinas.com.br/2025/10/procon-aponta-que-54-dos-idosos-tem-dificuldade-em-usar-aplicativos-para-compras-e-servicos/), and the well-documented role of WhatsApp family groups as the primary information channel [bbc](https://www.bbc.com/portuguese/brasil-43797257) — points to the same conclusion: **the elderly user runs the app, but the adult child discovers it, installs it, configures it, and pays for it**. Vigora's marketing, copy, screenshots, app store creative, and pricing pages should be written for the 35–55-year-old filho/filha worrying about a parent, not for the 65+ monitorado.

The fastest channel to that buyer is **paid social (Instagram/Facebook) targeting adult children + organic seeding inside family WhatsApp groups**. The fastest language is **"saber que está bem mesmo de longe" + "tranquilidade para a família"**, not "app para idosos." The price point Vigora has chosen (R$4,90/month, R$99,90 lifetime) sits in a defensible position: ~2× cheaper than Goldies and ~40× cheaper than hardware teleassistance, but anchored against a market where the adult child is comparing against R$80–R$150/month teleassistance and R$1,600+/month caregivers — so they are not price-sensitive in the way the absolute number suggests.

## 1. Market sizing: the audience exists and is large

The smartphone-ownership picture for Brazilian 60+ is dramatically better than the conventional wisdom about "idosos não usam tecnologia."

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|

| Internet use, 60+, 2024 | **69.8%** (up from 44.8% in 2019) | IBGE PNAD TIC 2024 [viva](https://viva.com.br/tecnologia/uso-de-internet-por-idosos-e-o-grupo-que-mais-cresce-mostra-pnad.html) |

| 75+ using internet daily or almost daily | **94%** | TIC Domicílios 2025 / Folha [folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/equilibrioesaude/2026/01/folha-estreia-serie-idosos-conectados-sobre-a-relacao-da-populacao-mais-velha-com-os-smartphones.shtml) |

| Android share, Brazil mobile | **~80–82%** | StatCounter [statcounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/brazil) |

**Implications for Vigora:**

- The audience is real and reachable on the same devices everyone else uses. There is no "elderly Brazilians aren't online" objection to absorb.
- **Android-first is correct.** Brazil's smartphone market is ~80% Android, with iOS revenue share much higher only because Android dominates by volume in lower-income classes (where Brazilian elderly are over-represented) [grandviewresearch](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/smartphone-market/brazil).
- Time on phone is high — peaks in morning and evening for 60+ (vs. distributed throughout the day for Gen Z) ^[consumidormoderno](https://consumidormoderno.com.br/60-anos-celular/ "Sem briga: quem tem mais de 60 anos também \"não sai desse celular\" ..."). Vigora notifications and check-in prompts should respect that two-peak rhythm.
- **The 2 in 10 who don't have a phone are the wrong customer.** 59.3% cite "inability to use it" and 24.1% "no necessity" [ibge](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/44032-no-brasil-88-9-da-populacao-de-10-anos-ou-mais-tinha-celular-em-2024). These are not addressable through a mobile app no matter how simple — they are the customers of hardware teleassistance (Viva, HelpCare) and home care services. Don't try to convert them.

**The chronic-conditions audience is even larger:** Brazil has ~40 million people 60+ and a hypertension/diabetes/cardiac patient base spanning younger ages too. The closest data point on category app penetration is Fiocruz's review finding that only 4 of 1,931 Portuguese-language elderly-targeted apps met rigorous quality criteria [brazilianjournals](https://ojs.brazilianjournals.com.br/ojs/index.php/BRJD/article/download/29832/23512) — suggesting supply is fragmented and underserved despite real demand.

## 2. Discovery channels: WhatsApp + adult-child Instagram dominate; "official" channels barely exist

**The channels that actually work for this audience are not the channels Brazilian healthtechs talk about wanting.** Here is the evidence-based ranking:

### What works

**WhatsApp family groups (highest priority).** WhatsApp is the dominant communication tool for 60+ Brazilians — 87% of 75+ users send instant messages daily [folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/equilibrioesaude/2026/01/folha-estreia-serie-idosos-conectados-sobre-a-relacao-da-populacao-mais-velha-com-os-smartphones.shtml). 51% of Brazilians share health information in family WhatsApp groups (vs. 32% in friend groups and 9% in colleague groups) [bbc](https://www.bbc.com/portuguese/brasil-43797257). When an adult child sends "mãe, instala isso" with a link, the elderly parent is more likely to install — and the child is more likely to walk them through setup over voice message — than via any other channel.

**Adult child on Instagram/Facebook.** Goldies' entire LinkedIn marketing positions adult children as the target ("O público-alvo são os filhos de idosos") [linkedin](https://br.linkedin.com/company/goldies-saude-integrada). The customer testimonials on Goldies' homepage are uniformly written from the adult child's perspective ("Com o app da Goldies, finalmente consegui organizar todas as informações de saúde da minha mãe em um só lugar… Agora, me sinto mais conectada e confiante de que ela está se cuidando bem, mesmo quando estou trabalhando") [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/). The 35–55-year-old worried about a 65+ parent is reachable through standard Meta ads — and converts more easily than the 65+ themselves.

### What doesn't work as well as expected

**Doctor and pharmacist referrals are not a real channel.** Despite Memed having 150,000 physicians on its platform and ~75% market share in digital prescriptions [memed](https://memed.com.br/), there is **no evidence of structured doctor-to-patient app referral programs** in Brazil. One Brazilian gerontologist independently recommends apps to her patients [viva](https://viva.com.br/tecnologia/longevidade-veja-5-aplicativos-que-ajudam-idosos-a-cuidar-da-saude.html), but this is individual practice, not an institutional channel. Goldies' "free for healthcare professionals" tier is an attempt to seed this channel but has produced ~1,500 downloads in 5 years  — meaning even with this strategy, the channel hasn't moved volume.

**Pharmacy chains compete, they don't refer.** Raia/Drogasil launched its own medication reminder feature inside Programa Mais Saúde ("Na Rotina") [drogaraia](https://www.drogaraia.com.br/programamaissaude); Drogasil's app has 350k–756k reviews. **Pharmacy chains are building their own apps with overlapping features, not pointing customers at third parties.** Do not plan on pharmacy-counter referrals as a primary channel.

**YouTube tech-teacher channels reach the audience but not for app discovery.** Channels like Canal do Seu Neto (Renie Gomes), "Sou 60," "60+," and "Avós da Razão" reach hundreds of thousands of Brazilian 60+ [canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/redes-sociais/acima-de-65-influencers-espalham-simpatia-ao-falar-sobre-envelhecimento-165592/) , but their content is "how do I use WhatsApp / how do I avoid scams" — not "you should try this health app." A sponsored integration on Canal do Seu Neto teaching elderly viewers how to set up Vigora step-by-step could work, but evidence of effectiveness is nonexistent (no Brazilian healthtech has publicly disclosed using this channel successfully).

**App-store search is unlikely to work.** Elderly Brazilians overwhelmingly don't install apps themselves — the adult child does. There's no documented evidence of elderly Brazilians browsing Play Store/App Store and selecting health apps independently [corasenior](https://corasenior.com.br/guia-pratico-com-aplicativos-uteis-para-o-dia-a-dia-dos-idosos/). ASO matters only insofar as the adult child searches "lembrete remédio para idoso" or "monitorar saúde mãe" — but those searches are the entry point, not the elder's.

### Channel strategy for Vigora

The implication is unusually clean: **build acquisition around the adult child as the buyer/installer**, with two stacked motions:

1. **Paid Meta (Instagram/Facebook) to 30–55-year-old Brazilians with elderly parents** — copywriting in adult-child voice ("Sua mãe mora sozinha?" "Saiba que seu pai está bem mesmo quando você está no trabalho"). This is the same playbook Goldies uses.
2. **WhatsApp-native sharing mechanics** — make it trivially easy for the adult child who installed Vigora to share a setup link with siblings/relatives via WhatsApp, and design the onboarding so a daughter can complete setup remotely (paid feature opportunity: "ajude um parente a configurar à distância").

Doctor/pharmacy partnerships are worth pursuing only if they are essentially free (free professional tier mirroring Goldies, white-label content for one geriatric clinic, etc.) — they will not drive launch volume.

## 3. The actual adoption barriers, in elderly Brazilians' own words

**The barriers are not abstract; they have been documented in Brazilian academic and journalistic studies and elderly users describe them in remarkably consistent language.** This matters because it tells you what copy and product decisions need to address.

### Barriers (in priority order by evidence weight)

**Complexity and password overload.** Direct quote from an elderly Brazilian in qualitative INSS access research: "Eu acho superdifícil. Às vezes, tem tanta senha, senha pra isso, senha para aquilo. Tem tantas especificações que a gente se perde. A gente dessa idade, ou está informado, bem informado, ou então é preferível que nem mexa" [agenciabrasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/direitos-humanos/noticia/2025-05/idosos-enfrentam-barreiras-digitais-para-acessar-servicos-do-inss). **Product implication: Vigora's onboarding must work without forcing the elder to create a password they'll forget. Magic-link-by-WhatsApp or "set-up by adult child, hand phone to parent" flows beat email/password every time.**

**Fear of making mistakes ("medo de errar").** Multiple Brazilian studies document this. Elderly report "receio de clicar, de fazer alguma coisa errada" [agenciabrasil](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/direitos-humanos/noticia/2025-05/idosos-enfrentam-barreiras-digitais-para-acessar-servicos-do-inss) and "medo de quebrar o celular ao explorar funções desconhecidas" [iesb](https://jornalismo.iesb.br/destaque1/golpes-virtuais-se-multiplicam-entre-idosos-e-expoem-impacto-da-exclusao-digital/). **Product implication: confirmation dialogs before any action, easy undo, and avoiding "destructive" UI patterns (sliders, swipe-to-delete).**

**Fear of scams.** Reddit testimony from adult children: an elderly father "virou um alvo fácil pra golpista" [reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/AgingParents/comments/1ohm30v/methods_to_monitor_parents_phone_usage/?tl=pt-br). Among 75+ Brazilians, golpe-de-Pix and false-bank-call scams are pervasive. **Trust-signal implication: explicit "Vigora never asks for bank info / Pix / passwords" copy in onboarding. Avoid any UI that resembles a banking app authorization screen.**

**Distrust of technology generally.** A FEBRABAN survey found 79% of Brazilians believe the elderly "não confiam ou não se sentem seguros" with digital tools; 80% of elderly themselves don't use applications due to "barreiras tecnológicas" [febraban](https://portal.febraban.org.br/noticia/3842/pt-br/) [record](https://record.r7.com/hoje-em-dia/video/estudo-revela-que-80-dos-idosos-nao-utilizam-aplicativos-devido-a-barreiras-tecnologicas-16122024/). **Implication: the adult child as installer is not optional — it is the primary unlock for the 80%.**

**Vision/dexterity issues.** Design guidance from Brazilian UX practitioners specifies "tamanhos de fonte maiores (mínimo de 16px)" and "botões grandes" as baseline requirements [virtualvision](https://www.virtualvision.com.br/blog/acessibilidade-terceira-idade/). **Vigora needs 16pt+ default font, high-contrast color scheme, button targets ≥48dp, and ideally a "modo simplificado" toggle.**

**Shame at asking for help.** Literature documents "vergonha de pedir ajuda" as a recurring emotional barrier [iesb](https://jornalismo.iesb.br/destaque1/golpes-virtuais-se-multiplicam-entre-idosos-e-expoem-impacto-da-exclusao-digital/). **Implication: in-app help should be findable without admitting "I don't know how" — e.g., a persistent "?" not requiring a settings dive.**

### What overcomes the barriers

- **Family member as co-installer.** Brazilian academic research repeatedly identifies the "rede de apoio familiar" as the primary mechanism for elderly digital literacy [upf](https://ojs.upf.br/index.php/rbceh/article/download/12819/114117436) [studiespublicacoes](https://ojs.studiespublicacoes.com.br/ojs/index.php/cadped/article/view/6302). This validates the "adult child installs and configures" flow as not just a marketing observation but a product requirement.
- **Patience and simplicity.** FEBRABAN found "confiança mútua, paciência e simplicidade no ensino" as key facilitators [febraban](https://portal.febraban.org.br/noticia/3842/pt-br/). The product needs to be teachable in 5 minutes by a non-expert family member.
- **Doctor/society validation.** Where it exists, it works — Elfie's marketing copy leans heavily on "validado pela Sociedade Brasileira de Cardiologia e Sociedade Brasileira de Diabetes" [oglobo](https://oglobo.globo.com/conteudo-de-marca/elfie/noticia/2023/11/07/aplicativo-empodera-pacientes-no-controle-de-doencas-cronicas.ghtml). Vigora doesn't have this yet, but a CRM/CRF endorsement or even a Sociedade Brasileira de Geriatria e Gerontologia (SBGG) mention would carry weight.
- **No registration required for basic use.** CPqD Alcance's "não há necessidade de cadastro" is cited as an explicit barrier-removal pattern [cicloassist](https://cicloassist.com.br/blog/7-aplicativos-para-idosos-que-simplificam-a-tecnologia-em-2025).

**On LGPD specifically:** Brazilian law treats health data as "dados pessoais sensíveis" with fines up to 2% of revenue (capped R$50M) for violations [medicinasa](https://medicinasa.com.br/dados-sensiveis-saude/), and the LGPD explicitly mandates that elderly data handling be "simples, clara, acessível e adequada ao seu entendimento" [lec](https://lec.com.br/lgpd-e-o-mito-do-consentimento-para-tratamento-dos-dados-de-saude/). **However, no source documents elderly users themselves raising LGPD concerns** — the regulatory anxiety lives with the family/institution, not the user. **Practical implication: Vigora's privacy page should reassure the adult child (the buyer), not lecture the elder. The visible privacy promise on the install flow should be in plain language and concrete: "seus dados de saúde só são vistos por quem você convidar."**

## 4. Language and framing: the words that work and the words that don't

This is the area where the evidence is most concrete and where Vigora's marketing has the most to gain or lose from precision.

### Magnet words (used by every successful Brazilian competitor and confirmed in user reviews)

| Word/concept | Why it works | Source examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Tranquilidade** | Central emotional promise across every competitor | Goldies, Viva, CuideMe, HelpCare [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/) [vivamonitoramento](https://vivamonitoramento.com.br/) |
| **Segurança** | Primary functional driver — paired with tranquilidade | All competitors [helpcarebrasil](https://www.helpcarebrasil.com.br/) |
| **Autonomia / Independência** | Reframes monitoring as freedom-enabling, not surveillance | Viva, CuideMe, TeleHelp [telehelp](https://blog.telehelp.com.br/pulseira-com-botao-de-emergencia-da-telehelp/) |
| **Simples / Fácil / Intuitivo** | Direct response to "medo de errar" | Caixa de Remédios reviews, Goldies testimonials [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/) |
| **Estar perto mesmo de longe** | The single most powerful adult-child frame | Goldies' "gestão de saúde da sua família mesmo que você esteja distante" [goldies](https://goldies.com.br/) |
| **Saber que está bem** | Captures the dead man's switch value proposition in one phrase | Goldies' "Como estou me sentindo" button name [playstore](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.goldies.app) |
| **Lembrete / Não esquecer** | Concrete pain — the only word that works for the medication frame | MyTherapy PT, Goldies, Caixa de Remédios [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/mytherapy-lembrete-de-rem%C3%A9dios/id662170995) |
| **Emergência / SOS / Resposta rápida** | Functional, urgent, paired with quantified time ("em menos de 1 minuto") | HelpCare [helpcarebrasil](https://www.helpcarebrasil.com.br/) |

### Friction words

| Word | Why it creates resistance | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Velho** | Pejorative — associated with "inutility and decline" | [metodosupera](https://metodosupera.com.br/velho-pessoa-idosa-ou-terceira-idade-como-falar-com-o-idoso/) |
| **Frágil / Dependente / Senil** | Pathologizing — implies the user is broken | [cmaissaude](https://cmaissaude.com.br/idoso-senior-ou-terceira-idade-qual-a-forma-correta-de-falar/) |
| **Terceira idade / Melhor idade** | "Superestima potencialidades" — feels patronizing | [b50](https://b50.com.br/como-se-referir-a-velhice-conheca-os-termos-mais-utilizados/) |
| **Idoso** (when used reductively as label) | Brazilian Congress and academic guidance increasingly prefer "pessoa idosa"; still legally standard but loaded when used as direct address | ^[camara](https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/800531-comissao-aprova-substituicao-de-termo-idoso-por-pessoa-idosa-em-cinco-leis/ "Comissão aprova substituição de termo \"idoso\" por \"pessoa idosa\" ...") |
| **Monitoramento** (without security context) | Feels invasive/surveillance-y when not paired with safety/autonomy framing | [telehelp](https://blog.telehelp.com.br/cuidado-a-distancia-para-idosos/) |

### What this means for Vigora's copy

**Lead with the adult-child frame, not the elder-as-target frame.** Instead of "Aplicativo para idosos" use "Para que sua família tenha tranquilidade." Instead of "Monitoramento de idosos" use "Saiba que ele(a) está bem mesmo de longe."

**The dead man's switch deserves its own headline.** The canvas already identifies this as Vigora's unique positioning. The competitor copy from Goldies ("Como estou me sentindo") shows that the manual version of this feature works — but Vigora's is automatic, which is more reassuring. Frame it as: "Se algo acontecer, sua família saberá em minutos — sem você precisar lembrar de avisar."

**Borrow Viva's testimonial structure.** Viva's most effective copy is a customer quote: "Moro sozinha e com o Viva me sinto tranquila. Já precisei acionar sem querer e fui atendida imediatamente. Isso dá muita segurança para mim e para meus filhos" [vivamonitoramento](https://vivamonitoramento.com.br/). Notice the two-audience structure ("para mim e para meus filhos") — Vigora needs the same.

**Borrow CuideMe's origin story.** CuideMe leads with "Inspirada na relação de uma neta com sua avó" [cuideme](https://www.cuideme.care/). Pedro's product was inspired by his stepdad's experience — that's a real founder story and it humanizes the brand for the adult-child buyer. Use it.

## 5. Willingness to pay: the data is sparse, but adjacent markets bracket the answer

**No published Brazilian survey directly measures WTP for elderly health/safety apps.** This is itself a finding — the category is too new for that research to exist. But adjacent pricing tells you what the buyer is already comparing against.

| Substitute / Comparable | Brazilian price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Goldies Básico (closest competitor) | **R$9,90/mo, R$104,90/yr** |  |
| Hora do Remédio (medication only) | R$7,99/mo | (canvas) |
| Alarme e Lembrete de Remédio (lifetime, meds only) | R$29,99 lifetime | (canvas) |
| Teleassistance (Viva, hardware + 24h call center) | **R$130–R$190/mo** | (canvas) |
| CuideMe (smartwatch + service) | R$690 setup + R$179,90/mo | (canvas) |
| In-home caregiver (cuidador domiciliar mensalista) | **R$1,600–R$2,800/mo** (São Paulo avg R$1,425) | [cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.com.br/quanto-custa/cuidado-idosos-domicilio) [cuidadosconecta](https://cuidadosconecta.com/blog/valor-cuidador-de-idoso-tabela-de-precos-2025) |
| Plantão 24h | R$4,500–R$8,000/mo | [cuidadosconecta](https://cuidadosconecta.com/blog/valor-cuidador-de-idoso-tabela-de-precos-2025) |

**Three implications:**

1. **Vigora's R$4,90/month is psychologically free relative to what families spend.** The adult child considering hiring a caregiver is comparing against R$1,600+/month — R$4,90 is rounding error. Vigora's pricing problem is not "too expensive" but "is it serious enough to trust?" Pricing too low risks signaling unserious product. Consider testing R$9,90/month (matching Goldies) to see if conversion holds.
2. **R$99,90 lifetime is the strongest hook for the subscription-fatigued buyer.** The Brazilian app market does show subscription fatigue — Medisafe's early-2026 paywall produced documented user revolt (56% 1-star reviews)  (canvas). And the lifetime tier is roughly equal to one year of Goldies. **The lifetime tier should be the default suggested plan in upgrade prompts**, not the monthly.
3. **Free trial conventions: 30 days is the Brazilian healthtech norm.** Goldies offers 30 days with all features active . Vigora's 7-day trial is short by this benchmark — consider lengthening to 14 or 30 days, especially given that elderly users (and their adult children doing setup) need time to internalize a routine before paying. **Credit-card-upfront friction is likely lethal** in a segment where 54% of elderly have general app usability anxiety [portalcbncampinas](https://portalcbncampinas.com.br/2025/10/procon-aponta-que-54-dos-idosos-tem-dificuldade-em-usar-aplicativos-para-compras-e-servicos/) — use a no-card trial if RevenueCat permits.

## 6. Caregiver as discovery vector — quick read pending deeper research

The brief asked for a "brief" treatment, but the evidence here is strong enough to state directly:

- **Goldies' explicit positioning is "filhos de idosos"** [linkedin](https://br.linkedin.com/company/goldies-saude-integrada). Every Goldies testimonial is structured from the adult child's voice. This is not subtle — the closest direct competitor has already concluded the buyer is the family.
- **The elderly aren't browsing the App Store.** Searches across Brazilian senior tech guides yielded no qualitative evidence of elderly self-discovery; guides consistently frame app installation as a family activity [corasenior](https://corasenior.com.br/guia-pratico-com-aplicativos-uteis-para-o-dia-a-dia-dos-idosos/).
- **WhatsApp is the install vector.** When the adult child says "instala isso, mãe" with a link, that's the install moment. 87% of Brazilians 75+ send instant messages daily [folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/equilibrioesaude/2026/01/folha-estreia-serie-idosos-conectados-sobre-a-relacao-da-populacao-mais-velha-com-os-smartphones.shtml) — the link will get opened.
- **Family-plan architecture is a competitive deficit.** Goldies offers a Família plan (up to 6 monitorados) for R$29,90/month . The canvas notes Vigora has not yet built equivalent multi-monitored architecture. Given the buyer is the family, this is a real gap — though it can be a follow-on feature rather than a launch blocker.

**One unresolved question worth investigating next:** What share of installs/purchases happen when the adult child installs Vigora on their own phone *first* (to see it before recommending) vs. installs directly on the parent's phone? This affects whether Vigora needs a "caregiver dashboard" account distinct from the "monitorado" account, and how onboarding flows should bifurcate. Goldies' multi-user architecture suggests this matters — but there's no published research on it for Brazil specifically.

## 7. Synthesis: what this means Vigora should do next

If the goal is to land first paying users in the 1–2 month runway, the evidence points to a concentrated playbook:

1. **Reposition all marketing copy for the adult child, not the elder.** Homepage hero: "Tranquilidade para você. Segurança para quem você ama." Subhead: "O único app brasileiro que avisa sua família se algo acontecer com seu pai ou mãe — sem depender deles avisarem." The dead man's switch is the lead feature, not a footnote among five.
2. **Lead distribution with paid Meta + WhatsApp share mechanics.** Doctor/pharmacy/influencer channels do not move volume for Brazilian healthtechs. Goldies has tried them for 5 years and shows ~1,500 downloads. The faster path is buying 35–55 demographic targeting on Instagram/Facebook with adult-child copy and making the install flow shareable in one WhatsApp tap.
3. **Rebuild onboarding around "adult child installs for parent."** Magic-link by WhatsApp, no password creation on the elder's phone, large fonts as default, "modo simplificado" toggle, no destructive UI patterns. The adult child should be able to complete most of setup themselves and hand the phone over for confirmation.
4. **Make the lifetime tier the suggested upgrade default**, not the monthly. Lengthen the trial from 7 to 14–30 days to match Goldies and the Brazilian healthtech norm. Don't ask for credit card upfront.
5. **Don't compete on price below R$4,90.** Vigora is already at the floor of the market and the buyer is comparing against R$1,600/month caregivers. The risk is signaling "too cheap to be serious," not "too expensive to consider." Test R$9,90/month against R$4,90/month to see if conversion holds — Goldies' price suggests it should.
6. **Build the family-plan / multi-monitorado feature within 90 days of launch.** Vigora's current architecture treats the elder as primary user; the buyer wants to manage one or more parents and possibly bring in siblings as authorized contacts. This is also a stronger LTV story than a single subscription.
7. **Pursue one SBGG or Brazilian Society of Cardiology endorsement** in the first six months. Elfie's example shows this pattern moves trust in a way no amount of marketing copy can [oglobo](https://oglobo.globo.com/conteudo-de-marca/elfie/noticia/2023/11/07/aplicativo-empodera-pacientes-no-controle-de-doencas-cronicas.ghtml). Pedro's stepdad's corporate network may unlock this introduction.

## Where additional research would most strengthen these conclusions

- **A small primary survey of 20–30 Brazilian adult children with elderly parents** would convert most of the inferred WTP and channel claims into directly-measured ones. The published research doesn't exist for this segment; primary research is faster than waiting for it to be published. Specifically: ask whether the adult child or the elder pays, what they currently spend on parent care, and what would make them subscribe to an app like Vigora.
